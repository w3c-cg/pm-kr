Demos

Each demo here is an individual contribution, offered as input to the W3C Procedural Memory Knowledge Representation Community Group. None is a report or a decision of the group.

  • Dynamic Procedural Texture Rendering: a 3D scene you can look at before its pictures have arrived. By Daniel Ramos.
  • The Key: walk an invented game studio’s headquarters, carry three keys, and see which doors they open. By Daniel Campos Ramos.
  • The Key 2: walk the studio’s headquarters with its people, carry keys from today’s internet, hold Knowledge3D’s tablet and compose services from several origins. This copy has no key server and no people, so its keys are represented; the whole world runs at zionbtnet.ddns.net/demos/the-key/. By Daniel Campos Ramos.
