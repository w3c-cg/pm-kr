The Key.

Walk the headquarters

People walk the hall, queue at the desk and turn to you when you come near. Try the doors with the keys you carry, hold the tablet, and place the services you choose.

Keyboard and mouse
WASD to walk, drag or press ← → to look. F Action. I opens the inventory: click an item, or Q and E then F; Use acts with it at once. Outside the inventory Q and E still change the item in hand. With the tablet held: R route, T size, X put it away.
Touch
The stick walks, a drag on the scene looks. The ring button opens the inventory: tap an item to take it. The round button is Action. Held sideways the text steps aside for the controls; hold the phone upright to read it.
Standard gamepad
Left stick to walk, right stick to look. Y opens the inventory: the left stick points at an item, A takes it, B closes. Left bumper previous item, right bumper next item, A or Cross for Action.
WebXR
Look at a station and press the trigger for Action. Move with the stick. B or Y opens the inventory: point the ray at an item and pull the trigger. A left squeeze selects the previous item and a right squeeze the next. Without controllers, the keys work as above.

What the keys stand for

Each key says on itself which mechanism is real here: OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, OpenID Connect, WebAuthn with a security key or a fingerprint, and a TLS client certificate run for real against the local test server. Threshold, Circuit and Relay are represented only.
