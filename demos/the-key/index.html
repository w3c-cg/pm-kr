The Key.

Walk the headquarters

Try the public lobby, visit the account desk, then ask Mica to deliver a parcel.

Keyboard and mouse
WASD to walk, drag or use ← → to look, E to use, 1–3 to choose a key.
Touch
Use the arrows to walk, drag the scene to look, tap a key and Use.
Standard gamepad
Left stick to walk, right stick to look, A or Cross to use, right bumper to change key.
WebXR
Look at a station and press the trigger. Use a stick to move and squeeze to change key.

What the keys stand for

A passkey answers only to the site that issued it. A grant has a scope and a lifetime. A mandate lets a helper act within the limits you give it.

All three are simulations in this demo.
