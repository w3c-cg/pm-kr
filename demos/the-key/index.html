The Key.

Walk the headquarters

People walk the hall, queue at the desk and turn to you when you come near. Try the doors with the keys you carry, hold the tablet, and place the services you choose.

Keyboard and mouse
WASD to walk, drag or press ← → to look. Q previous item, E next item, F Action. With the tablet held: R route, T size, X put it away.
Touch
Tap arrows to walk, drag to look. Tap Previous or Next, or an item in the bar, then Action.
Standard gamepad
Left stick to walk, right stick to look. Left bumper previous item, right bumper next item, A or Cross for Action.
WebXR
Look at a station and press the trigger for Action. Move with the stick. A left squeeze selects the previous item and a right squeeze the next.

What the keys stand for

Each key says on itself which mechanism is real here: OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, OpenID Connect, WebAuthn with a security key or a fingerprint, and a TLS client certificate run for real against the local test server. Threshold, Circuit and Relay are represented only.
