Walk the headquarters

Try the public lobby, create a pretend game-store account at the desk, then ask Mica to deliver a parcel.

Keyboard and mouse
WASD to walk, drag or press ← → to look. Q previous key, E next key, F Action. The 1–3 keys select directly.
Touch
Tap arrows to walk, drag to look. Tap Previous key, Next key or a key, then Action.
Standard gamepad
Left stick to walk, right stick to look. Left bumper previous key, right bumper next key, A or Cross for Action.
WebXR
Look at a station and press the trigger for Action. Move with the stick. A left squeeze selects the previous key and a right squeeze selects the next.

What the keys stand for

A passkey answers only to the site that issued it. A grant has a scope and a lifetime. A mandate lets a helper act within the limits you give it.

All three are simulations in this demo.
