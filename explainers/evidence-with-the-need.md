# Take evidence with the need

**Status:** an individual's input to the Procedural Memory Knowledge Representation Community Group, by Daniel Campos Ramos, 6 November 2026.
It is not a decision or a report of the group.
Comments and objections are welcome in this repository's issues.

## The question

W3C is building a way for developers to raise a need before they have a proposal ([Mind the gaps, TPAC 2026](https://github.com/w3c/tpac2026-breakouts/issues/68)).
A need is easier to judge when there is something to test.
What should the intake ask for, so that the people reading it can see the gap on their own screens instead of taking it on trust?

## The idea

Three things with every need:

1. **the need**, in the end user's words;
2. **the workaround** sites build today, because a workaround shows the cost and the shape of the missing piece;
3. **a page that shows it**, live, so a reviewer can open it on their own device.

For stereo, the workaround is a site drawing its own side by side and anaglyph modes, and the live pages are a public list anyone can check on a 3D TV, a headset or a monitor with red and cyan glasses ([Mind the gaps input, points 3 and 4](../talks/2026-10-27-tpac-mind-the-gaps/README.md#3-a-workaround-each-site-has-to-build)).

## Where it exists today

- The public list of live 3D and HDR sources, kept as issues so anyone can add to it: [w3c-cg/pm-kr#4](https://github.com/w3c-cg/pm-kr/issues/4), with YouTube channels and portals in [#5](https://github.com/w3c-cg/pm-kr/issues/5), VR, WebXR and author-made stereo in [#6](https://github.com/w3c-cg/pm-kr/issues/6), and HDR, gain maps and deep colour in [#7](https://github.com/w3c-cg/pm-kr/issues/7).
- Brian Kardell's reaction in the session, recorded in the [minutes](https://www.w3.org/2026/10/27-mind-the-gaps-minutes.html): origin trials should give high-quality feedback on the intake proposal.

## What to try

Open one page from [#6](https://github.com/w3c-cg/pm-kr/issues/6) on a screen you own and say what you saw: both views, one view, or two flat halves.
That one line is evidence.

## Questions for the group

1. Should the intake form ask for a workaround and a live page?
2. Which need of yours has a page that shows it?
