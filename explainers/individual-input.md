# A label for individual input

**Status:** an individual's input to the Procedural Memory Knowledge Representation Community Group, by Daniel Campos Ramos, 6 November 2026.
It is not a decision or a report of the group.
Comments and objections are welcome in this repository's issues.

## The question

A Community Group's deliverables are what its participants decide together.
Much of what moves a group, though, arrives as one participant's input: a report, a page of points for a session, an explainer like this one.
The Community Group redesign's proposed statuses describe group work.
Is there a place in the design for input the group has not adopted, shown as clearly as a specification's maturity ([Community Group tools input, point 4](../talks/2026-10-28-tpac-cg-redesign/README.md#4-a-label-for-individual-input))?

## The idea

Three lines at the top of every such document, in the same words each time:

> **Status:** an individual's input to the Procedural Memory Knowledge Representation Community Group, by [name], [date].
> It is not a decision or a report of the group.
> Comments and objections are welcome in this repository's issues.

A report rendered with ReSpec says the same in its status section and carries `specStatus: "unofficial"`, so the group's own report style is never borrowed by a document the group has not adopted; the repository's check profile allows no other status for those reports ([reports and their status](../reports/README.md)).
The label does two things.
It protects the group: nothing speaks for it that it did not decide.
And it protects the input: it can be bold, early and wrong without putting the group's name on it, and the group can later adopt it by a recorded decision.

## Where it exists today

- Every page in `talks/`, the two stereo reports, the glossary and every explainer here carry the label ([standards process words](../glossary/README.md#2-standards-process-words), "individual input").
- The first decision asked of the group in that form: whether to adopt the [Synthetic User](synthetic-user.md) explainer as a group work item, which the group edits from there, with replies on the list by 14 November ([public-pm-kr, 31 October 2026](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-pm-kr/2026Oct/0022.html)).
- The Community Group's own tool, respec-mcp, enforces the status per profile ([The group's rules in its repository](rules-in-the-repository.md)).
