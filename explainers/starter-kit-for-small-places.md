# A starter kit for small places: a café, a shop, a small business

**Status:** an individual's input to the Procedural Memory Knowledge Representation Community Group, by Daniel Campos Ramos, 15 November 2026.
It is not a decision or a report of the group.
Comments and objections are welcome in this repository's issues.
**Why in PM-KR:** a place built from procedures is knowledge stored once, which a person and an AI read the same way.

## The question

Today, putting a small place on a Web of Worlds takes someone who knows the whole stack: modelling, textures, a scene format, a viewer, hosting, and now AI.
A café owner has none of that, and should not need it.
What would let a small business be there on the first day?

## The idea

A starter kit: generic defaults a person can start from.
It is not the most detailed place, and it is not tailored to anyone.
It is enough to be open, and everything in it can be changed later.

1. **Places.** A café, a shop, a small office: each a plain scene with its rooms, counter, shelves and door.
2. **Textures as procedures.** Walls, floors, wood, tiles and plaster come as small recipes computed on the visitor's device, so the place shows at once on a slow connection, and photographs sharpen it when they arrive.
3. **Their own place, scanned.** Where the person wants their real room, a video of it becomes a 3D scan that takes the place of the ready pieces.
4. **Actions that show themselves.** The door, the menu and the till each say what they do, where they are, to a person and to an assistive tool.
5. **An assistant from the first day.** An AI works there as a Synthetic User: declared, under that person's mandate, bounded and revocable, with that person answering for it.

The person chooses a place, changes the names and the pictures, and is open.
Nothing in it needs an autonomous agent or a specialist.

## Free kits, and a market of experts

The basic kits are free.
People who know the craft build on them and charge for tailoring and for advanced features, as happened with WordPress themes.
So the kit does not replace the experts: it gives them customers who are already open.

## Where it exists today

- Textures as procedures: the [Dynamic Procedural Texture Rendering demo](https://w3c-cg.github.io/pm-kr/demos/procedural-texture-rendering/), a courtyard drawn from 15.5 KB of procedures in a plain glTF 2.0 scene.
- Actions that show themselves: [Actionable knowledge representation](actionable-knowledge-representation.md).
- The assistant: [Synthetic User](synthetic-user.md).
- The tools to make and view it in 3D: [Sparky Stereo OS](https://github.com/Sparky-OS/sparky-stereo-os), an open system with its creative programs. Scanning a place from a video is planned there.
- The kit itself does not exist yet. This page is the proposal.
